# Product intent — current

Help visitors quickly understand Julie’s role and work, explore evidence of her contribution, and reach résumé/contact information. Make the experience welcoming, thoughtful, creative and easy to navigate; those are intended qualities, not measured user outcomes.

Use the current record-player sketchbook composition. The introduction, player and bottom navigation remain coordinated groups; header and weather/time have reserved space. Preserve clear text, keyboard/touch access, predictable focus and reduced motion. Avoid forcing content into one viewport when doing so compromises readability or controls.
