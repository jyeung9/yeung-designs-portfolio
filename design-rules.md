# Portfolio design rules — November 2, 2026

The approved current local version is the source of truth. Use [design-system.md](design-system.md), [pattern specifications](references/patterns-nov02.md) and [visual references](references/visual-components.md) together. When older documents conflict, these current specifications prevail.

- Preserve the gridded sketchbook, handwritten labels, tactile wood/metal player and frosted navigation/weather surfaces.
- Coordinate introduction, player and navigation as groups. Reserve header and utility space. Use shared tokens and measured clearances, not independent viewport-specific offsets.
- Keep functional type at least 16px and targets at least 44px. Current playback targets are 56/84/56px. Scroll/reflow when space is insufficient.
- Normal text targets 4.5:1 with explicit surface pairs in every theme. Plain handwritten hover hints stay near the pointer, clear boundaries and remain inside the viewport. Avoid boxes, text halos and blend modes.
- Preserve keyboard/touch actions and reduced-motion handling. Music starts paused.
- Preview material visual/behavioral changes locally before asking Julie to approve them. Update accepted specifications and publish only with authorization; never treat a preview capture as approval.

Earlier directions remain [historical evidence](references/history/pre-evening-nov02-design-rules.md), not active instructions.
